Our story

A working family farm, not a factory.

We're the Corder family, and we've spent years learning what our little corner of Louisiana can give — if you tend it patiently.

The bees came first: a few hives, a lot of stings, and eventually honey worth putting our name on. The dairy grew up right alongside them — Jersey cows raised gently, milked with care, and treated like the members of the family they are.

Everything we make comes off this land and goes to real neighbors. No middleman, no mystery. Just food you can trace back to the hands that raised it.

How farm-to-table works here

From our hands to your table, in three steps.

Step 01

We raise & harvest

We keep the hives and milk the herd ourselves. Nothing gets outsourced, and nothing leaves the farm we wouldn't feed our own family.

Step 02

You order direct

Tell us what you'd like right here on the site. Your request comes straight to us — no store shelves, no distributors in between.

Step 03

Fresh to your table

We confirm your order and arrange local pickup or delivery, so it reaches you about as fresh as food can get.

Good food takes time. We've got the time.
