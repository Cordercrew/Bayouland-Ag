100% Pure

Nothing added. Nothing taken away.

Our bees forage the wildflowers of south Louisiana, and what they bring back is what goes in the jar. We don't heat it down or filter out the good parts — you get honey the way it comes out of the comb, with all its local character intact.

Because it's raw and local, no two harvests taste exactly alike. That's the point.
