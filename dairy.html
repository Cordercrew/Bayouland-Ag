The old way still works.

Since 2013

A small herd, raised with patience.

Bayouland Creamery started with a handful of Jersey cows and a simple idea: keep the herd small enough that every animal gets real attention. More than a decade later, that's still exactly how we run it.

Our cows graze, get named, get spoiled a little, and give us rich Jersey milk in return. When you buy from us, you're buying from a family that mucks the stalls and knows every cow in the pasture.

Opportunity is often missed because it's wearing mud boots and looks like hard work.
